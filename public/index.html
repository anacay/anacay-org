Anacay spends its days on AI. Here it supports the human side: open communities where people learn from each other, share what they know and build something together. Nothing here is for sale.

How Anacay supports open work

  • It gives time, hosting and a home on the web.
  • It doesn’t get control over what each effort says, a place to sell, or anyone’s data.
  • It says so wherever its own interest touches the work.
